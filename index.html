03

Dip Analysis & Remediation

The five usual suspects · your remediation plan

For every noticeable dip, identify which of the five usual suspects are contributing. It's almost always more than one — the combination is what matters. Then write the remediation. Specific. Actionable. Owned.

PATTERN 01
Cash Tied Up in Inventory
Stock you have paid for that has not sold yet. Every dollar of product on a shelf is cash already spent and not available.
PATTERN 02
Front-Loaded Costs
You spend to produce or to advertise before the customer pays — money out the door ahead of the money coming back.
PATTERN 03
Growth Cash Consumption
Hiring ahead of revenue, or buying more inventory to support growth. Both drain cash even as the business gets healthier.
PATTERN 04
Debt Service Timing
Fixed payments landing at the worst point in your monthly cycle.
PATTERN 05
Owner Draws at the Wrong Time
Personal income drawn during a mid-month trough, even when the business is profitable over the full month.

A dip is any noticeable drop you choose to flag. There's no automatic rule — your judgment is the diagnostic.
